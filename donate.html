Why It Matters

Your Support
Changes Lives

Hope Family Care Center has been a cornerstone of health on Kansas City's east side. We serve patients who are uninsured, underinsured, and underserved — offering primary care, pediatrics, women's health, same-day care, and on-site lab work.

Your donation helps ensure that cost is never a barrier to getting the care a family needs.

  • Access — helps a family see a provider when they have no insurance
  • Ongoing care — supports neighbors managing diabetes, blood pressure, and asthma
  • Affordability — keeps sliding-scale fees within reach for patients who cannot pay full price
  • Monthly gifts create lasting, sustained impact in our community

Your Impact

Where Your Gift Goes

Primary Care

Preventive checkups, chronic disease management, and same-day care for adults and children — regardless of insurance status.

Social & Community Support

Our social work team helps patients apply for benefits and connect with food, housing, transportation, and language services.

Women & Children

Prenatal care, well-child visits, and women's health services that keep Kansas City families healthy from the start.

Appointment & Medication Support

Help keeping appointments and staying on prescribed medications, so a treatment plan actually works.
