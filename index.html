Our Care

Comprehensive care,
all in one place

Family medicine for every age, plus the support that helps care actually happen.

Primary Care

Wellness exams, preventive screenings, and ongoing care for chronic conditions.

Pediatrics

Newborn care, well-child visits, and immunizations for growing families.

Women's Health

Annual gynecologic exams, family planning, and prenatal guidance.

Same-Day Care

Same-day appointments for new illness and minor injury. Walk-ins welcome during clinic hours.

For life-threatening emergencies, call 911.

Laboratory Services

On-site blood work, metabolic panels, and rapid strep and flu tests.

Community & Social Support

Help connecting to food, housing, transportation, and benefits programs.

About Hope Family Care

Quality care for our
Kansas City neighbors

Hope Family Care Center was founded on a simple mission: to honor God by providing quality, personal health care to our Kansas City neighbors.

Many people in our community face barriers to housing, transportation, food, and medical care. HOPE is here to walk alongside you and connect you with practical support — caring for the whole person: body, mind, and spirit.

Accreditation & Recognition

Recognized for Quality

HOPE takes part in the federal Health Center Program. Here is what that means for the care you receive.

Most recent · 2025
Certification of Compliance

Issued following the Health Center Program's 2025 Operational Site Visit — a federal review of how the clinic operates, from clinical quality to governance.

Federal Health Center Program

We meet the federal Health Center Program requirements for quality and safety, and we set fees on a sliding scale based on what you can afford.

HRSA Supported

HOPE participates in the Health Center Program administered by the Health Resources & Services Administration, the federal effort to widen health care access in underserved communities.

Recognized for Quality

HRSA named HOPE an Advancing Health Information Technology for Quality awardee in both 2023 and 2024 — recognition for using technology to improve patient care.

Beyond the Exam Room

Health is more than
what happens in a clinic

Healthcare is only one part of being well. Our team also helps connect patients with trusted housing, food, transportation, benefits, and community resources.

Housing

Referral & navigation

We connect you with local organizations that provide housing assistance, and help you follow through.

Food

Referral & navigation

We help you find food pantries and nutrition programs near where you live.

Transportation

Care coordination

Getting here is often the hardest part. Tell our staff and we will help you work out how.

Benefits

Application help

Our staff can help you check eligibility and apply for Medicaid and other benefit programs.

Our role here is connection and coordination. We help you apply, make referrals to trusted local organizations, and follow up — so you are not working through any of it alone.
